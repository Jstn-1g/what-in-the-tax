2026 residential tax example
In 2026, the residential tax rate is 1.432260%.
For a home valued at $405,000 in Waterloo, this works out to $5,800.65.
Of that total:
City of Waterloo receives 30%
Region of Waterloo receives 59%
your school board receives 11%
