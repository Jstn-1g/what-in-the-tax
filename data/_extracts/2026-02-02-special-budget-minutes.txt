Councillor Tilley inquiries about ensuring funds are set aside for communications. It is 
noted that communications are an operating expense and it is an ongoing effort by staff 
to reach as many people as possible. 
Councillor Rolleman inquiries about costs associated with having a windrow attachment 
put on the snow plow trucks. It is noted by staff that at this time, the costs associated with 
delivering this service is unknown. It is noted that staff will prepare a follow report that will 
look at expanding the Township snow removal service level to include windrow clearing 
and will include an analysis of logistics, potential service level adjustments and costs to 
assist with forecasting future operating and capital budgets. 
Councillor Wilms inquiries about the road repaving projects current listed in the capital 
budget. 
Councillor Wilms inquiries about the pickleball courts and cricket pitches and whether 
staff need to factor in land acquisition costs for it to be built, or whether they will be 
placed in parks located in new development.

financial analysis
