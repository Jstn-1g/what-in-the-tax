11.2 REPORTS 
11.2.1 Report No. BLE-04-2026: Firearm Discharge By-law Update. 
Comments from Council: 
Councillor Tilley inquired about what changes were made to respond to the public 
comments received during the public consultation phase. It is noted that the Draft By-law 
was amended to only include the settlement areas of Clyde, Branchton, Roseville and 
Ayr. It is further noted that the draft By-law was amended to align better with provincial 
hunting regulations for setbacks from roadways and recreational trails. 
Councillor Wilms noted her concerns regarding treating bows the same as firearms, and 
her concerns regarding the lot sizes cited in the By-law. Councillor Wilms noted that 
other Township By-law's allow certain activities with appropriate lot sizes. 
Councillor Tilley noted that he would prefer to have a minimum lot size requirement to 
allow people to discharge firearms in settlement areas, and noted that he suggests the 
word weapon be removed from the draft by-law. Councillor Tilley also noted his concerns 
with the definition of a firearm. 
Councillor Rolleman noted that the Township has received complaints regarding firearm 
discharge in the settlement areas.

16.BUSINESS ARISING FROM CLOSED SESSION 
Mayor Foxton provided an oral update. A review of CAO Report No. 13-2026 was 
conducted. This Report and the ensuing discussion was a follow-up to Council's review 
of this matter at the February 23rd and March 23rd, 2026 Council lnCamera Meetings. 
The Report is associated with the on-going work in support of the Pay Equity/ Wage 
Compensation Study being completed for the non-union personnel of the Township . 
Pesce & Associates was retained by the Township and has been assisting Council and 
senior staff assess various models and options with respect to wage compensation for 
the non-union personnel. This work commenced in November 2025. 
Arising from the work that has unfolded since the commencement of the Study in the Fall 
of 2025 through to the present period the work program is now at a decision point. To 
this end, Council will now entertain a Resolution. 
C-161-26 Moved by Councillor Tilley 
Seconded by Councillor Ostner 
THAT Council implement a new wage structure and grid system for the 
Non-Union Personnel that will be effective July 1st, 2026. 
AND THAT the Treasurer be authorized to fund an expenditure of 
$88,332.00 with the requisite dollars being drawn from the Pay Equity/ 
Wages Reserve Account; 
AND THAT Council adopt the Salary Administration Policy [HR-01 O] for 
Non-Union Personnel, effective July 1st, 2026; 
AND THAT the Chief Administrative Officer coordinate the notification to 
each Non-Union Staff member as to how the outcomes from the Pay Equity 
I Wage Compensation Study as completed by Pesce & Associates and the 
associated implementation date of July 1st, 2026 affects them on an 
individual basis. 
CARRIED
