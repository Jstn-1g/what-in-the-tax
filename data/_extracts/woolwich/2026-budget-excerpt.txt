Township of Woolwich 2026 Budget Book (excerpt) + mayor news release
Sources:
https://www.woolwich.ca/media/pytjesxk/2026-budget-book-final.pdf
https://www.woolwich.ca/news/posts/mayor-shantz-highlights-balanced-approach-in-2026-township-of-woolwich-budget/

The 2026 Budget proposes a 7.8% total tax increase. This increase is made up of 6.8% base tax levy and a 1% addition to the infrastructure levy.

The approved budget results in an increase of $99.83 annually for the average household, or $8.32 per month.

Note: Township documents compare rates using average assessment value but do not print a single numeric average CVA in the excerpts retained here.
