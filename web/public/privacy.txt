Taxpayer Receipt preview — privacy notice

This is a static, independent research preview. It does not ask for an address,
owner name, roll number, account, or contact details. It has no accounts,
advertising, behavioural analytics, runtime AI, or tracking pixels.

Any assessment scenario you enter is calculated in your browser and is not sent
to this project. A plain-language display preference may be stored in your
browser. You can remove it by clearing this site's browser storage.

The hosting provider necessarily receives technical request information such as
an IP address, browser details, requested path, and time. Persistent Cloudflare
Workers Logs are disabled in this project's deployment configuration. Hosting
providers may still maintain security, abuse-prevention, or aggregate operational
records under their own policies.

Links to municipal documents and other evidence leave this site. Those external
sites have their own privacy practices. This preview sends no referrer header
when supported by the browser.

No publisher contact or correction desk has yet been designated. Until both
exist, this preview must not be represented as a Published taxpayer receipt.
